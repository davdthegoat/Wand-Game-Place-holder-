Hello Hackclub staff!
We're excited to present to you "Wandgame"! (title subject to change). 

In our game the protagonist decides to tidy up and organize a long-abandoned shop of a dying trade as a hobby, uncovering the secrets and trivia left behind by the previous shop owner, Mr. Ollivander.

Week 1:
The game is in very early stages of development right now as we are trying to figure out some basics of working as a group and using group tools such as github as well as learning Blender and Krita.
We hope you won't judge us to harshly for there is a lot more to come :). We also had a mishap with github earlier which you can see on: [https://github.com/davdthegoat/Wand-Game-Place-holder].
Also due to technical difficulties, we were unable to add our design assets onto the Unity game engine, we sincerely hope that these Lapse recordings suffice, 
and are willing to provide more than ample screenshots if necessary.

The controls are simple: "E" for picking up items, "R" for rotating items held in hand, "F" to place in a item in shelf, left click while holding an item to throw it.

We hope you enjoy "Wandgame" in it's early stages.
